## The Notes Between Us

*The Notes Between Us* is my current novel project set in the MAGIS universe, a world where music is more than sound — it’s power, identity, and connection. This page will grow as the book does, collecting character notes, worldbuilding details, and excerpts.

### About the Story

*The Notes Between Us* follows Basil O’Brian, a music‑obsessed, eyeliner‑smudged, My‑Chemical‑Romance‑devoted teenager who’s convinced the world doesn’t really “get” him — until a mysterious envelope arrives in the mail. Inside is a song that shouldn’t exist and an invitation to MAGIS: Misfits Achieving Greatness in Song, a school where musical talent isn’t just encouraged, it’s amplified into something strange, powerful, and a little bit magical.

Basil’s mom is thrilled. His dad is skeptical. But the letter is real, the opportunity is huge, and the brand‑new guitar his dad buys him feels like a quiet vote of confidence he didn’t expect.

At MAGIS, Basil meets the bandmates who will become his closest friends — a group of weird, brilliant, chaotic musicians who push him creatively, challenge him emotionally, and force him to figure out who he is when he’s not hiding behind sarcasm and a band tee. As Basil learns how MAGIS works, he discovers that music here isn’t just performance; it’s identity, connection, and a force that can shape the world around him.

The story blends humor, heart, and musical magic — exploring creativity, belonging, and what happens when a kid who’s always felt like an outsider finally finds the place where he fits.

### Characters
### Characters

This section introduces the main cast of *The Notes Between Us* — Basil, his family, his bandmates, and the faculty who shape his journey at MAGIS.

---

#### Basil O’Brian
A music‑obsessed, eyeliner‑smudged, My‑Chemical‑Romance‑devoted teenager who feels most himself with a guitar in his hands. Sarcastic, sensitive, and convinced he’s misunderstood, Basil’s world shifts when he receives the MAGIS letter. He’s the emotional center of the story — messy, creative, and learning how to belong.

---

### Family

#### Mom
Warm, supportive, and quietly proud. She sees Basil’s talent clearly and encourages him to take the MAGIS opportunity. She’s the emotional anchor at home.

#### Dad
Skeptical at first, but deeply invested in Basil’s future. His gift of the guitar becomes a symbolic moment — a gesture of belief he struggles to express in words.

#### Sis
Basil’s younger sister, sharp‑eyed and observant. She teases him, supports him, and sees through his emo‑kid theatrics more than anyone else. Their relationship adds humor and grounding to Basil’s home life.

---

### Bandmates

#### Colton
Basil’s upright‑bass‑playing friend from back home. Steady, rhythmic, and quietly brilliant, Colton brings a grounded musical presence to the band. He’s the kind of guy who taps out patterns on the table without realizing it, and the kind of friend who shows up even when he doesn’t know what to say.

#### Tyrone
Sharp‑eyed, sharp‑minded, and always keeping time with something only he can hear. Tyrone’s internal rhythm never shuts off. He’s confident without being cocky, intense without being intimidating, and becomes one of Basil’s closest friends almost by accident.

#### Sam (Samantha)
Warm, bright, and effortlessly musical. Sam hums melodies under her breath that sound like chart‑toppers, even when she’s not trying. She’s supportive without being soft, honest without being harsh, and becomes a key emotional anchor in Basil’s MAGIS experience.

---

### MAGIS Faculty

#### Ms. Bass
Precise, disciplined, and sharper than the edge of a metronome. Ms. Bass teaches theory and foundational technique, and she expects excellence from every student who walks into her classroom. Her approval is something you earn, not something you’re given.

#### Mr. Forte
Loud, energetic, and impossible to ignore. Mr. Forte treats every lesson like a performance, booming enthusiasm across the room whether students are ready for it or not. He pushes Basil and his classmates to play bigger, think bigger, and take risks they wouldn’t take on their own.

#### Fogerty
The headmaster of MAGIS — mythic, grounded, and carrying the kind of presence that makes a room go quiet without him asking. Fogerty’s intro speech sets the tone for the entire school: you’re here for a reason, anyone can make noise, but the chosen few make magic.

#### Buckethead
The enigmatic guitar instructor whose teaching style is equal parts brilliance and chaos. Buckethead communicates more through riffs than words, and his lessons often feel like puzzles Basil has to solve. He’s unpredictable, intimidating, and occasionally hilarious — the kind of mentor who forces students to discover things they didn’t know they were capable of.
